How this MVP works

Single local model call, schema cleanup, smart layout selection, then figure rendering.

  1. Extract pipeline steps from research text into strict JSON.
  2. Validate and normalize nodes, labels, sections, and flow connections.
  3. Pick a layout, draw a polished SVG figure, and export it as SVG or PNG.
